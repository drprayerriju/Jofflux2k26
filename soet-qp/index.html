How to enter equations

Enclose each equation in $…$. Use braces for grouped powers and subscripts. The preview below each question shows the result.

$\frac{M}{I}=\frac{\sigma}{y}=\frac{E}{R}$$\delta=\frac{P L^{3}}{3 E I}$$v=\sqrt{2 g h}$$R_{A}+R_{B}=100$

Supported: fractions, square roots, powers, subscripts, Greek symbols, sin/cos/tan/log/ln, ∫ and ∑ symbols. Matrices, cases and full LaTeX environments are not supported. Complex equations can be added in Word after download. Word may use its maths font for equation symbols.
